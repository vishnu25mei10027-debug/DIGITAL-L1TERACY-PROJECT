✅ Stay Safe Online — Checklist for College Students (India)
- Use strong, unique passwords for each account (mix of letters, numbers, symbols). Avoid using your name, DOB, or simple patterns.
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on email, social media, and banking apps for extra security.
- Verify emails and links before clicking — check the sender’s address carefully, especially for “urgent” messages.
- Download apps only from official stores like Google Play Store or Apple App Store to avoid malware.
- Never share OTPs, passwords, or PINs with anyone—even if they claim to be from a bank or college authority.
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities like banking or logging into important accounts.
- Limit personal information on social media (phone number, address, college ID, etc.) to reduce risk of misuse.
- Regularly update your devices and apps to fix security vulnerabilities.
- Financial / UPI Safety Tips
- Double-check UPI IDs and QR codes before making payments—fraudsters often create similar-looking IDs.
- Never approve “collect requests” (payment requests) unless you are sure of the sender—receiving money does NOT require approval.
- If You Become a Victim
- Report immediately on National Cyber Crime Portal
- Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930 for quick assistance

Staying alert and following these steps can significantly reduce your risk of cyber fraud.
